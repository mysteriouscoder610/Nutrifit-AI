Mediterranean Diet — A Practical Guide

Overview
The Mediterranean diet is built around the traditional eating patterns of countries bordering the Mediterranean Sea (Greece, Italy, Spain, southern France). It emphasizes plant foods, healthy fats, moderate fish and dairy, and minimal red meat or refined sugar. It is consistently rated among the world's healthiest dietary patterns and is associated with lower cardiovascular risk, healthier weight, better insulin sensitivity, and improved cognitive aging.

Core Principles
1. Vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts, and seeds form the base of every meal.
2. Olive oil is the primary fat — used in cooking, dressings, and finishing dishes.
3. Fish and seafood are eaten 2-3 times per week. Oily fish like salmon, sardines, mackerel are encouraged.
4. Poultry, eggs, cheese, and yogurt are consumed in moderation.
5. Red meat is limited to a few times per month.
6. Sweets are occasional, not daily.
7. Water is the main beverage; red wine is optional and only if culturally appropriate.

Sample Daily Plan
- Breakfast: Greek yogurt with walnuts, honey, and fresh berries; whole-grain toast with olive oil.
- Lunch: Lentil and vegetable soup; mixed greens with feta, tomatoes, olives, cucumber, and olive oil dressing; whole-grain pita.
- Snack: A handful of almonds and a piece of fruit.
- Dinner: Grilled salmon with roasted vegetables (zucchini, bell pepper, eggplant) and quinoa; side of leafy greens.

Nutritional Highlights
- High in monounsaturated fats (from olive oil and nuts) and omega-3s (from fish).
- Rich in fiber from whole grains, legumes, vegetables, and fruits.
- Naturally moderate in sodium when home-prepared.
- Provides ample antioxidants (vitamin C, E, polyphenols).

Tips for Indian Households
- Substitute olive oil with cold-pressed mustard oil or extra-virgin olive oil where possible.
- Replace pita with whole-wheat roti or millet roti.
- Include local greens (palak, methi, amaranth) and pulses (moong, masoor).
- Use paneer in moderation in place of feta; opt for low-salt cottage cheese.
