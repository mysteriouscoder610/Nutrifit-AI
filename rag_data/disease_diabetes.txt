Diabetes (Type 1 and Type 2) — Nutrition Guide

Goals
- Maintain blood glucose in the target range (typically fasting 80-130 mg/dL, post-meal under 180 mg/dL).
- Achieve healthy weight; even 5-7% weight loss meaningfully improves Type 2 outcomes.
- Reduce cardiovascular risk through fiber, healthy fats, and limited sodium.
- Prevent hypoglycemia, especially in Type 1 or insulin-treated Type 2.

Foods to Emphasize
- Non-starchy vegetables (leafy greens, cucumber, tomato, capsicum, beans, broccoli, cauliflower, lauki).
- Whole grains and millets: jowar, ragi, bajra, brown rice, oats, quinoa.
- Lentils and pulses: moong, chana, masoor, rajma, chickpeas (a fiber + protein boost that blunts the post-meal glucose spike).
- Lean proteins: skinless chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, paneer (in moderation).
- Healthy fats: olive oil, mustard oil, nuts, seeds, avocado.
- Low-glycemic fruits: apple, pear, papaya, guava, berries, citrus — paired with protein/fat.

Foods to Limit or Avoid
- Refined sugar, jaggery, honey in significant amounts; sugary drinks; sweetened lassi.
- White bread, polished white rice, maida (refined flour) preparations.
- Deep-fried snacks (samosa, pakoda, vada).
- Packaged juices and "diet" cookies — they spike glucose despite marketing.
- Processed and cured meats (high sodium, high saturated fat).

Plate Method (No Counting)
- Half plate non-starchy vegetables.
- One quarter lean protein.
- One quarter whole grains or starchy vegetables.
- A small portion of fruit or dairy on the side.

Carbohydrate Counting (For Insulin Users)
Match insulin dose to grams of carbohydrate. Use food labels and standard portion charts. A typical adult dose is 1 unit rapid-acting insulin per 10-15 g carbs, individualized.

Hypoglycemia Treatment (Rule of 15)
If blood sugar drops below 70 mg/dL, take 15 g fast-acting carbs (3 glucose tablets, 4 oz juice, or 1 tbsp honey), wait 15 minutes, recheck, repeat if still low.

Sample Day for Type 2
- Breakfast: Vegetable besan chilla + mint chutney + 1 small bowl curd.
- Mid-morning: 6-8 almonds + 1 small apple.
- Lunch: 2 jowar rotis + 1 cup palak paneer (light) + cucumber raita + green salad.
- Snack: Cup of green tea + roasted chana.
- Dinner: Grilled fish + sautéed broccoli and beans + 1/2 cup brown rice.
- Bedtime (if needed): 1 cup unsweetened soy milk.
