PCOS, Thyroid Disorders, and Hormonal Health — Nutrition Guide

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)
PCOS is driven largely by insulin resistance and chronic low-grade inflammation. Diet that improves insulin sensitivity is the cornerstone of management.

Recommended Pattern
- Low-glycemic, high-fiber meals: legumes, whole grains, non-starchy vegetables, berries, nuts.
- 25-35 g protein per meal to stabilize blood sugar and reduce cravings.
- Healthy fats: olive oil, ghee in moderation, avocados, fatty fish, walnuts and flax for omega-3s.
- Limit refined carbs and added sugar — these worsen insulin resistance and acne.
- Anti-inflammatory spices: turmeric, ginger, cinnamon.
- Spearmint tea (2 cups/day) may help reduce excess androgens (modest evidence).

Foods to Limit
- Sugary drinks, mithai, refined-flour items.
- Ultra-processed snacks and trans fats.
- Excess dairy in those with breakouts (individual response varies).

Lifestyle
- 150 min/week of aerobic activity + 2 strength sessions improve insulin sensitivity dramatically.
- 7-9 hours of sleep; chronic short sleep worsens insulin resistance.
- Stress reduction (yoga, meditation) lowers cortisol and supports cycle regularity.

Hypothyroidism
Goals: support thyroid hormone production and manage weight despite a slowed metabolism.

Helpful Nutrients
- Iodine: iodized salt, dairy, eggs, fish (avoid mega-doses from kelp supplements).
- Selenium: 1-2 Brazil nuts/day, eggs, sunflower seeds.
- Zinc: pumpkin seeds, lentils, chickpeas, beef.
- Iron: leafy greens, beans, lean red meat, fortified cereals.
- Vitamin D: sun exposure plus supplements if deficient.

Cautions
- Goitrogens (raw cruciferous vegetables, soy in excess) can interfere if iodine is low — cooking neutralizes most of the effect; moderate intake is fine.
- Take levothyroxine on an empty stomach, 30-60 minutes before food, calcium, or iron.

Hyperthyroidism
- Increase calorie and protein intake to offset hypermetabolism.
- Limit iodine-rich foods and supplements.
- Avoid excessive caffeine (worsens palpitations).

Sample Day for PCOS
- Breakfast: Vegetable moong dal cheela + mint chutney + 1 cup green tea.
- Snack: Apple + 1 tbsp peanut butter.
- Lunch: Quinoa pulao + grilled paneer/chicken + cucumber raita + salad.
- Snack: 1/2 cup roasted chana + green tea or spearmint tea.
- Dinner: Grilled fish or tofu + sautéed mixed vegetables + 1 small jowar roti + sliced avocado.
