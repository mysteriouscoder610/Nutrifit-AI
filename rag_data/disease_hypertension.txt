Hypertension — Nutrition Guide

Targets
- Blood pressure under 130/80 mmHg for most adults; under 140/90 for older adults without comorbidities.
- Sodium under 1,500-2,300 mg/day.
- Potassium 3,500-4,700 mg/day from food (unless renal disease).
- Healthy weight, regular aerobic activity (150 min/week), limited alcohol.

Best Eating Pattern: DASH or Mediterranean
Both emphasize fruits, vegetables, whole grains, low-fat dairy, lean proteins, nuts, and unsaturated fats while restricting sodium, saturated fat, and added sugar.

Foods to Emphasize
- Leafy greens (spinach, methi, lettuce), beetroot, tomato, garlic.
- Bananas, oranges, melons, berries, apricots.
- Beans, lentils, chickpeas.
- Low-fat dairy or fortified plant milks.
- Fatty fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel) twice a week for omega-3s.
- Whole grains, oats, quinoa, brown rice, millets.
- Unsalted nuts and seeds.

Foods to Limit
- Salt and salted snacks (chips, namkeen, papads, pickles).
- Cured and processed meats; restaurant food.
- Sugar-sweetened beverages and energy drinks.
- Excess caffeine in sensitive individuals.
- Alcohol — at most 1 drink/day for women, 2 for men.

Cooking Strategies
- Use lemon, vinegar, garlic, ginger, herbs and salt-free spice blends.
- Cook from scratch and avoid stock cubes / packaged sauces.
- Read labels: aim for under 5% DV sodium per serving.

Sample Day
- Breakfast: 1 cup oatmeal with banana and walnuts + skim milk.
- Snack: A guava and 6 almonds.
- Lunch: Brown rice + dal + mixed vegetable sabzi + curd + salad.
- Snack: Roasted chana + green tea.
- Dinner: Baked salmon or tofu + steamed broccoli + sweet potato + leafy salad.

Lifestyle Reinforcers
Daily 30-minute brisk walk, weight loss if BMI > 25, smoking cessation, stress management, and consistent sleep (7-8 hours) all directly lower blood pressure.
