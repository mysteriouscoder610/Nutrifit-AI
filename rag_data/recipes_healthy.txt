Healthy Home-Cooked Recipes — Step by Step

1. Moong Dal Cheela (high-protein breakfast, ~210 kcal, 14 g protein per cheela)
Ingredients: 1 cup green moong dal (soaked 4 h), 1 inch ginger, 2 green chilies, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp salt, chopped onion, tomato, coriander, 2 tsp ghee.
Method:
- Drain soaked moong dal and grind with ginger, chili, cumin, and a splash of water to a thick batter.
- Stir in chopped onion, tomato, and coriander.
- Heat a non-stick tawa, brush lightly with ghee, pour a ladle of batter and spread thin.
- Cook 2 minutes per side until edges crisp. Serve with mint chutney and curd.

2. Quinoa Veggie Bowl (one-pot lunch, ~420 kcal, 18 g protein)
Ingredients: 1 cup quinoa (rinsed), 2 cups water, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 cup chickpeas (cooked), 1 cup chopped vegetables (bell pepper, zucchini, carrot), 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp salt, lemon juice, fresh parsley.
Method:
- Toast quinoa for 2 minutes in olive oil, add water, simmer covered 12 minutes until fluffy.
- In a separate pan, sauté vegetables and chickpeas with cumin and paprika for 6 minutes.
- Combine, finish with lemon juice and parsley.

3. Tandoori Chicken Salad (high-protein dinner, ~380 kcal, 38 g protein)
Ingredients: 200 g chicken breast, 2 tbsp curd, 1 tsp tandoori masala, 1/2 tsp ginger-garlic paste, mixed salad greens, 1/2 cucumber, 1/2 tomato, 1 tbsp olive oil, lemon, mint chutney.
Method:
- Marinate chicken in curd, tandoori masala, ginger-garlic paste, and salt for 30 minutes.
- Grill on a hot pan for 5 minutes per side until charred and cooked through. Slice.
- Toss greens, cucumber, tomato with olive oil and lemon. Top with chicken and drizzle mint chutney.

4. Overnight Oats with Chia (make-ahead breakfast, ~340 kcal, 12 g protein)
Ingredients: 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1 tbsp chia seeds, 3/4 cup low-fat milk or almond milk, 1 tsp honey, 1/2 banana sliced, 1 tbsp almond butter, cinnamon.
Method: Mix all ingredients in a jar, refrigerate overnight. Top with banana and almond butter before eating.

5. Palak Paneer Light (~280 kcal per serving, 18 g protein)
Ingredients: 200 g spinach, 100 g paneer cubed, 1 small onion, 1 tomato, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, 1/2 tsp jeera, 1/4 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp salt, 1 tsp ghee, 2 tbsp curd.
Method:
- Blanch spinach 1 minute, plunge in cold water, blend to a smooth puree.
- Heat ghee, splutter jeera, sauté onion till translucent, add ginger-garlic, then tomato and spices.
- Add spinach puree, simmer 5 minutes, stir in curd off the flame.
- Add paneer cubes (do not deep-fry), simmer 2 minutes. Serve with multigrain roti.
